How to read this report
This is independent residential monitoring from one fixed terrace below the LPPT approach corridor. It is designed to show repeatable local exposure, not to replace certified airport noise mapping.
Source
ADS-B flight data paired with station SPL readings.
Limit
Current microphone can cap near 100 dB; peaks may be understated.
Purpose
A public record for patterns, outliers and night-time disturbance.
