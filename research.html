the archive

Everything below comes from the same independent station behind the rest of this site — one fixed terrace under the LPPT approach path, no official airport data mixed in. Free to reuse for coursework, papers or your own analysis. If you publish something with it, we'd genuinely like to hear about it.

Why this exists
Lisbon Finals has always been a citizen-science project first — a single sensor, openly measuring, rather than a claim of official authority. Opening the raw exports is the natural next step: anyone teaching or studying airport noise gets real, dated, local measurements instead of having to take our on-site summaries at face value.
Before you open the archive
One question: why does this data interest you?

It helps us understand who uses the work of a volunteer-run station — and, if you'd like, lets us notify you when new months of data are added. No account needed.

No account, no password, no name required. Nothing here is sold or shared — see the CC BY 4.0 license below.

Using this data
License & attribution
Released under CC BY 4.0 — reuse, adapt and redistribute freely, with credit. A line like Data: Lisbon Finals (lisbonfinals.com) in your methodology section or references is enough.
Worth knowing before you crunch the numbers
This is one consumer-grade sensor (IEC 651 type 2, ±1.5dB manufacturer tolerance) on one terrace, not a calibrated reference network — treat it as local, independent evidence, and cross-check anything load-bearing for your own conclusions.
Questions about the data, or planning to use it for a course or a paper? Write to chronos@lisbonfinals.com — always happy to hear what it's used for.
