Looking for a Co-Pilot

For a while now, "the network" has been something we talked about but couldn't point to. That changed this week: host-a-node.html is live, the front door for anyone who wants to run a station of their own, somewhere else, under their own flight path.

It lays out what that actually takes: a pre-flight checklist (Pi, RTL-SDR, sound meter, the rest), two ways to get there (build it yourself or have us ship it turnkey), and the honest limits: two nodes per airport, remote access to the unit only, never to your network. Still mostly a plan. But now it's a plan someone can actually apply to.

Alongside it, some quieter housekeeping: a batch of Captain's Log entries were quietly 404ing behind the scenes, fixed. The co-pilot callout on this very page was rendering nearly 300px tall on phones, fixed. And Chronos, who fields the press questions, now knows about all of it without being told by hand.

If you live under a flight path and this sounds like your kind of trouble, the checklist is one click away.
