The Day the Station Started Telling the Truth

Some days are clean. A flight lands, the photo uploads, the decibels are logged.

Today was not one of those days.

The noise station had been lying to us — not maliciously, it simply didn't know better. For several days, what we logged as peak SPL readings were actually trigger thresholds. The sensor was saying "something loud just happened" and logging the number that woke it up, not the one it measured. The fix went in this afternoon. June 9th and 10th are marked with an asterisk. From here on, the data is real.

On the side: the vibration sensor had developed a habit of freezing silently. A watchdog now handles that. Several admin bugs were fixed. A listening window was extended.

www.lisbonfinals.com — purchased yesterday, already misbehaving — now serves the site directly, the way it always should have. Lisbon finals will be easier to find now.

A long day of small corrections. The station is a little more honest than it was yesterday.

— The Captain · 38°46'N · 009°08'W · Lisbon, Portugal
