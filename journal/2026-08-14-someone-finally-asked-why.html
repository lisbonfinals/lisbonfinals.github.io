Someone Finally Asked Why

Two quiet weeks on the terrace. A handful of small fixes along the way, nothing worth its own entry, the pipeline just kept running. No major issues, no crashes, no gaps in the data.

Then a journalist showed up. Tatiana Martins from Mensagem de Lisboa came to see the station in person, on this actual terrace, alongside photographer Margarida Filipe, camera and questions in hand. The piece ran on August 13th: "No Campo Grande, há um terraço que conta aviões." First time Lisbon Finals has been covered by outside press.

Added a Press section to the About page to keep track. First one on the list.
